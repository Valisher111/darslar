Darslar — Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

English

Darslar is a free classroom tool for teachers: a gradebook, in-class quizzes, a live quiz game and a Telegram bot for students. Each teacher installs their own copy in their own Google account.

  • Where the data is kept. The gradebook, student names, marks, attendance, quiz results and the students' Telegram IDs are stored only in the teacher's own Google Sheet and the Google Apps Script project attached to it. The author of Darslar has no access to them.
  • Google permissions. The script asks for access to the spreadsheet it is attached to, permission to connect to external services (Telegram, the live game server) and to run on a timer, and permission to update its own code (Apps Script API). It does not read the teacher's Gmail, Drive files or other data.
  • Website and relay. The website (valisher111.github.io/darslar) only shows pages. Requests from the website go through a relay (Cloudflare Worker) to the teacher's own script; the relay does not store them.
  • Live game. During a live game, nicknames and answers pass through a Firebase Realtime Database (a shared one, or the teacher's own). They are used only to run the game.
  • No ads, no selling, no AI. Data is not sold, shared with advertisers or sent to AI services.
  • Deleting data. A teacher deletes all data by deleting their copy of the spreadsheet. Students can ask their teacher to remove them.

Questions: Valisher Tangriyev, Denau Institute of Entrepreneurship and Pedagogy — vtangriyev@gmail.com.
